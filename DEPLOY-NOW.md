# 🚀 DEPLOY TO NETLIFY IN 30 SECONDS

## Step-by-Step:

### 1. Open this link in your browser:
```
https://app.netlify.com/drop
```

### 2. Locate your folder:
Your camera app files are in:
```
/home/user/Frontier-cam-
```

### 3. Drag the folder:
- Find the `Frontier-cam-` folder on your computer
- Drag the ENTIRE folder into the Netlify Drop zone
- It will upload: index.html, README.md, and config files

### 4. Get your URL:
- Netlify will instantly give you a URL like:
  `https://silly-name-123456.netlify.app`
- Copy this URL

### 5. Open on iPhone:
- Open Safari on your iPhone
- Paste the URL
- Tap "Enable Camera"
- Start shooting! 📸

---

## ✅ That's it!

No login required for testing. The URL will stay active for free.

If you want a custom URL later:
- Sign up for free Netlify account
- "Claim this site"
- Change URL to something like `my-leica-cam.netlify.app`
