CONTACT SHEET

This is an homage to the legendary Fujifilm Frontier SP500 — the professional lab workstation that defined how photographers perfected their prints. Like a pro lab tech at their station, you have complete control over color balance, density, and film character.

Canvas Interactions

  • Single tap View original unedited photo (shows for 1 second)
  • Double tap Zoom to fill/cover canvas (tap again to zoom out)
  • Pinch Zoom in/out
  • Drag while zoomed Pan around the zoomed photo
  • Tap counter value Directly edit Bloom, Grain, or CMYK values

Navigation

  • Photo counter Tap to open contact sheet view
  • Contact sheet SELECT button enables batch delete or share Tap any photo to jump to it
  • Arrow buttons Navigate between photos in sequence
  • Gallery icon Return to upload screen
  • More menu Access COPY PASTE SHARE and GUIDE

Film Profiles

Start by choosing a film profile to get 95% there instantly

  • SUPRA Consumer color negative Classic portrait film with lifted shadows and warm skin tones
  • CHROME Slide film High saturation deep blacks punchy colors like Velvia
  • XXX Kodak Tri-X 400 B&W Legendary black and white film with punchy contrast lifted shadows and enhanced red sensitivity Perfect for street photography and portraits
  • 250D Cinema color negative Warm cinematic look used in Hollywood productions
  • HANDY CAM MiniDV tapes Early 2000s digital camcorder look with saturation crush and warm midtones

CMYK Fine-Tuning

After selecting a profile use CMYK sliders for final tweaks

  • CYAN Add cool tones or remove warmth
  • MAGENTA Add pink or purple cast
  • YELLOW Add warmth or golden hour vibes
  • DENSITY Master exposure control Negative values darken positive values brighten

Pro tip Small adjustments of ±2-3 make a big difference Tap value to clear or set precise amount

Brightness and Tone

Precision tonal adjustments for perfect exposure and mood

  • BRIGHTNESS Overall image brightness Lifts or lowers all tones equally
  • CONTRAST Separation between light and dark Positive values increase punch negative values soften
  • SHADOWS Control dark areas only Lift blocked shadows or deepen blacks
  • HIGHLIGHTS Control bright areas only Recover blown highlights or brighten light tones

Pro tip Use Shadows and Highlights for targeted adjustments without affecting the entire image

Bloom and Grain

  • BLOOM Optical diffusion Softens and fades bright areas creating dreamy overexposed film look
  • GRAIN Film texture More grain equals more character Tap value to reset or adjust

Quick Recipe

  • 1 Pick a film profile
  • 2 Adjust DENSITY if too dark or bright
  • 3 Tweak CYAN or YELLOW for color temperature
  • 4 Add BLOOM for softness
  • 5 Add GRAIN for texture
  • 6 Export or COPY settings to other photos
